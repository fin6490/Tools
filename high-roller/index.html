THE

Meridian

a casino story

Your uncle Sal left you a silver dollar, a dented flask and a debt to a loan shark. Work your way from a dive bar to the high-limit salon, and find out what really happened at Sal's last hand. Then wake up, in a city in the rain, and find out what really happened to you.

What's inside

  • Nine table games with real rules: blackjack (up to three hands), Ultimate Texas Hold'em, Mississippi Stud, Three Card Poker, Let It Ride, baccarat, craps, roulette and Casino War, with generous side bets and progressives.
  • A walkable town: casinos, a diner, a pawn shop, a motel and a steakhouse, all with something to find inside. Hunger, thirst and sleep keep the days honest.
  • The Seventeenth Card: a four-act mystery with clues, testimony, ciphers and a vault code, plus a trail of postcards from someone signing only "17".
  • Characters who talk back: chat with dealers, rivals, bartenders and suspects in your own words (and out loud, if you like). AI chat is included: up to 100 messages a day.
  • Slots, darts and a poker room, plus a career that runs from $500 to a billion, and a loan shark's back office for when you feel lucky.
  • Three levels: the dream town, the Meridian (a casino resort in a city that never stops raining, drawn in 3D), and a small room you'll wish you hadn't found. Each one changes what the last one meant.

Play money only: there is no real-money gambling, and nothing you win can be cashed out. Your progress is saved on your device.
